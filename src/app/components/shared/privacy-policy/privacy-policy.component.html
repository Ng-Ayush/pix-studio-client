My Studio App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 17, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how our My Studio App collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Photos: Uploaded or selected images shared by customers for album creation.
  • User details: Basic identification such as name, email, and contact information (optional and only if entered).
  • Device info & logs: Device model, OS version, IP address, app usage data, and crash logs to improve app performance.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To allow customers to select preferred photos from a session.
  • To allow photographers to receive selected photos for album designing.
  • To manage and track photo selections associated with each session.
  • To improve app functionality and troubleshoot issues.

3. How We Store Your Data

Your data, including photos, is securely stored in cloud storage services compliant with standard data protection protocols. We ensure restricted access and encrypted storage wherever applicable.

4. Sharing of Data

Data is only shared between the customer and their respective photographer. We do not share your data with third-party advertisers or unrelated entities.

Firebase Privacy Policy

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your selected photos.
  • Withdraw your selection or session at any time before final album creation.
  • Request correction of any associated personal information.
  • Request access to the data we have stored.

7. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Photo selections and related session data are stored for up to 90 days after final album creation, unless deleted earlier upon request.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

9. Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of significant changes through the app or via email.
